Package 02 · Mid-Tier

Submit your existing design documentation and I return a structured gap analysis report — identifying non-conformances, missing deliverables, and corrective actions before your design review.

How It Works

01

Brief Call

We spend 20–30 minutes on a video or voice call. You describe the project scope, applicable operator standard, and what documentation you need. I confirm what I need from you and provide a fixed-price quote.

02

You Send the Inputs

You share the project information packet — scope narrative, operator standard reference, any existing drawings or data sheets. A one-page summary is enough for most Package 1 scopes.

03

I Deliver

You receive a clean, formatted Word document within the agreed turnaround. Every deliverable includes a cover sheet, revision log, and references to the applicable standard clauses — ready to submit.

04

You Review & Revise

You mark up your comments and return them. I incorporate all revisions within 48 hours. Premium packages include a walkthrough call to ensure you understand every section before submitting to your client.
